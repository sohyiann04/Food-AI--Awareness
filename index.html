Understanding the Threat

AI-assisted phishing is not an entirely new type of cyberattack. Instead, Generative AI can make existing social engineering techniques faster, more scalable and potentially more convincing.

📧 Traditional Phishing

  • Generic messages
  • Obvious spelling or grammar errors
  • Limited personalisation
  • Smaller-scale campaigns
  • Easily recognisable warning signs

🤖 AI-Assisted Phishing

  • Personalised communication
  • More natural language
  • Multilingual content
  • Rapid message generation
  • Multiple variations of the same lure

How Generative AI Amplifies Phishing

Gen-AI can support different parts of a social engineering campaign.

✍️

Generation

Produces natural-sounding messages and business-style communication.

🎯

Personalisation

Helps adapt communication to a person's role, context or organisation.

🌍

Translation

Supports communication across different languages.

⚡

Automation

Reduces the time required to prepare large numbers of messages.

📈

Scaling

Enables attackers to create many message variations.

🎭

Impersonation Support

Can help imitate communication styles associated with trusted individuals.

Customer information and business correspondence.

Key Vulnerabilities

AI-assisted phishing can exploit a combination of human, technical and organisational weaknesses.

🧠 Human Vulnerabilities

Urgency, authority, familiarity, curiosity, lack of awareness and over-trust in professional-looking communication.

🔐 Technical Vulnerabilities

Password-only authentication, insufficient email filtering, weak URL protection and excessive account privileges.

🏢 Organisational Vulnerabilities

Weak verification procedures, limited security training, unclear reporting processes and insufficient Gen-AI policies.

AI-Assisted Phishing Lifecycle

A simplified defensive model showing where Generative AI may influence the threat process.

01

Information Gathering

Attackers may gather publicly available information about an organisation, departments and potential targets.

02

Target Selection

A target may be selected based on role, access level or expected authority.

03

AI-Assisted Content Preparation

Generative AI may assist with language, personalisation, translation and message variations.

GEN-AI INVOLVEMENT
04

Phishing Delivery

The deceptive communication may be delivered through email, SMS, messaging platforms or voice.

05

Victim Interaction

The victim may click a link, open an attachment, disclose information or respond to a request.

06

Account or Information Compromise

Stolen credentials or information may enable unauthorised access or further social engineering.

07

Organisational Impact

Potential consequences include financial loss, data exposure, operational disruption and reputational damage.

Detection, Prevention & Risk Treatment

Effective protection requires a layered combination of technical, human and organisational controls.

🔐

Technical Controls

  • Multi-factor authentication
  • Phishing-resistant authentication
  • Email filtering
  • Malicious URL scanning
  • Endpoint protection
  • Identity monitoring
👥

Human Controls

  • Security awareness training
  • Independent verification
  • Phishing simulations
  • Suspicious message reporting
  • Secure password practices
🏢

Organisational Controls

  • Gen-AI acceptable-use policy
  • Payment verification
  • Least privilege
  • Incident response plan
  • Regular policy review

Potential Impacts

A successful phishing incident can affect more than a single employee.

💰 Financial

Fraudulent transactions, payment diversion, account takeover and incident recovery costs.

⚙️ Operational

System access disruption, investigation, account suspension and delayed business processes.

🔒 Privacy

Exposure of customer, employee or business information.

⚖️ Legal & Compliance

Potential regulatory, contractual and data protection consequences.

📢 Reputation

Loss of customer trust, partner confidence and organisational credibility.

🌏 Societal

Increased exposure to scams and reduced trust in digital communication.

Interactive Phishing Example

Can you identify the warning signs? This is a fictional awareness example.

From: FoodHub Finance <finance@foodhub-payment-secure.example>
Subject: URGENT: Supplier Payment Verification Required

Dear Finance Team,

We have detected an issue with your supplier payment account. Immediate verification is required.

Please review your account using the following link:

Verify Payment Account

Failure to complete verification today may result in payment delays.

Regards,
FoodHub Finance Department

⚠️ Warning Signs Detected

• Artificial urgency
• Unexpected financial request
• Suspicious sender domain
• Request to click a link
• Pressure to act immediately
